## Black Knight Controller

**Black Knight Controller** is an open infrastructure automation project from AuzieTek focused on orchestration, observability, and AI-assisted operations.

The project started as a way to simplify host deployment, configuration templates, and infrastructure management. It has since grown into a broader platform concept: a control plane for deploying, managing, monitoring, and eventually helping operate modern infrastructure across virtual machines, containers, Kubernetes, and automation tools.

### What Black Knight Is

Black Knight Controller is designed to help engineers organize and automate infrastructure using Git-friendly templates, repeatable workflows, and extensible integrations.

It is not meant to replace system administrators, DevOps engineers, or SREs. It is meant to amplify them by reducing repetitive work, improving visibility, and creating a foundation for intelligent operations.

### Core Ideas

- **Template-driven automation:** Define reusable configurations and deployment patterns.
- **Host and node management:** Add, organize, and manage infrastructure components consistently.
- **Git-friendly operations:** Keep automation logic, configuration, and environment definitions version controlled.
- **Observability-first design:** Integrate with monitoring, logging, dashboards, and alerting tools.
- **AI-assisted workflows:** Explore how AI can help plan, troubleshoot, document, and eventually remediate infrastructure issues.
- **Open foundation:** Keep the project free and open for personal, educational, and non-commercial use.

### What Has Changed

The original Black Knight concept focused heavily on configuration management, deployment templates, and integration with tools such as Ansible, Puppet, Docker Swarm, Grafana, Kibana, Logstash, and Elasticsearch.

Those ideas are still important, but the project has evolved. Black Knight is now being shaped as a broader autonomous infrastructure platform, connecting traditional automation with modern DevOps, Kubernetes, observability, and AI operations.

### Current Development Focus

- Improving the core orchestration engine
- Building repeatable lab deployment workflows
- Expanding container and Kubernetes support
- Improving monitoring and dashboard integrations
- Adding better documentation, examples, and demos
- Exploring AI-assisted infrastructure planning and remediation

### Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to grow Black Knight Controller from an open source project into a sustainable ecosystem. That may include consulting, managed services, commercial support, hosted services, training, research, and enterprise integrations.

AuzieTek’s broader goal is to become a software company and think tank focused on autonomous operations, open infrastructure, observability, and practical AI for systems engineering.

### Story Behind the Name

The name **Black Knight** is inspired by the Black Knight satellite legend, a story often associated with mystery, advanced technology, and signals from beyond the ordinary.

For this project, the name represents an ambitious idea: infrastructure that can observe, respond, coordinate, and assist from above the noise of day-to-day operations. Not magic, not science fiction, but practical automation moving one step at a time toward intelligent systems.

### Why Support It

Sponsorship helps fund development hardware, lab infrastructure, cloud services, AI tools, subscriptions, documentation, testing, and the time required to keep building.

Every contribution helps move Black Knight from a personal engineering project toward a serious open platform for the next generation of infrastructure operations.
